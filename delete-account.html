Delete your account

In Sway: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and everything in it — reminders, settings, groups you own, friends — from our servers straight away, and clears it from your phone.

Can’t open the app? Email swayadhd@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, and we’ll delete your account and its data within 7 days.

Deleting doesn’t cancel a subscription — do that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Questions or requests? Write to us at swayadhd@gmail.com
